Signing up for Medicare is mostly a matter of timing. Your Initial Enrollment Period lasts seven months, and missing it can mean a late enrollment penalty that lasts as long as you have coverage. The decisions in between, Original Medicare or Medicare Advantage, and which drug plan, are easier when your paperwork is ready before the window opens.

Work through the checklist below in order. Check items off as you go (your progress saves in this browser), or download the one-page PDF to keep with your papers.
