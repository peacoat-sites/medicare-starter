Choosing how you get your Medicare coverage is a big decision, and Medicare Advantage (Part C) is one of the most popular paths. But "popular" doesn't mean "right for everyone." The plans bundle benefits in a way that fits some lifestyles beautifully and frustrates others.

This quick 7-question quiz looks at your habits, your priorities, and how you like to handle your care, then gives you a sense of how well a Medicare Advantage approach might suit you. It takes about two minutes, and there are no right or wrong answers, just your honest reflections.
