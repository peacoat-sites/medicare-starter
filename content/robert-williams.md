Robert Williams is trained through the SHIP counselor framework and focuses on helping lower-income seniors access the full range of benefits available to them, including Extra Help with prescription costs, Medicare Savings Programs, and Medicaid coordination. At Medicare Advisor Guide, he covers low-income benefit programs, avoiding common enrollment penalties, and getting help navigating Medicare when the process feels overwhelming.
